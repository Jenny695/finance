Investor research website. This free preview stores its accounts, catalog and reports on ephemeral local disk; rebuilds may reset data. No demo market data is generated.

Set `ADMIN_USERNAME` as a Variable, `ADMIN_PASSWORD` and `IFIND_REFRESH_TOKEN` as Secrets in Space Settings. The app uses the Space's `SPACE_HOST` for its public HTTPS origin. Open the direct `https://…hf.space` address in a separate tab to log in; this app does not allow embedding in a third-party frame.
